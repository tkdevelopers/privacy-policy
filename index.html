Privacy Policy for ScreenShare Shield

Last updated: October 19, 2026

ScreenShare Shield is a Chrome extension that helps protect your privacy while you share your screen. This policy explains what the extension does and does not do with your data.

Data we collect

ScreenShare Shield does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The extension has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no remote servers.

Data stored on your device

Your settings are saved locally in your browser using Chrome's storage feature. This includes whether Share Mode is on, your blur list of websites, and your panic hotkey. This information stays in your browser. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can remove it at any time by uninstalling the extension.

Website access

To blur websites on your blur list and to show a gray cover when you press the panic hotkey, the extension needs permission to run on web pages. It uses this access only to apply the blur or cover on your screen. It does not read, copy, store, or transmit the content of the pages you visit.

Third parties

We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any.

Changes to this policy

If the extension changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.
